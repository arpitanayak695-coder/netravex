The digital trust challenge

Trust is built at every hand-off.

Every day, your bank handles personal information across many touchpoints: a person’s name, phone number, account and KYC-related records, employee details, service requests and digital activity. Managing this information with care builds trust and keeps your bank DPDP-ready.

Personal data moves through five hand-offs, from the moment it is collected to the day it is deleted:

Collect

A customer applies or updates details.

Use

Teams use information to provide a service.

Share

Vendors or partners may receive limited data.

Store

Records sit in systems, files or backups.

Delete

Data is removed or anonymised when no longer needed, subject to applicable requirements.

Rights of people

Rights that are easy to exercise, transparent and well-documented.

The DPDP Act empowers individuals with clear rights over their personal data. Co-operative banks must make these rights easy to exercise, transparent and well-documented.

Access information

Ask what personal data is held and how it is used.

Correction, completion or update

Ask for inaccurate or incomplete data to be corrected or updated.

Erasure under applicable conditions

Ask for data to be erased where the applicable conditions are met.

Withdraw consent

Change your mind about a consent previously given.

Grievance redressal

Raise a concern and have it addressed through a clear channel.

Nominate another person

Name someone to exercise rights on your behalf where applicable.

Rights depend on the applicable legal ground, process and statutory exceptions. Exact requirements should be validated with qualified advisers and current notifications.
