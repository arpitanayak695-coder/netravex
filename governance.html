HELPS DECIDE / DO
  • Set privacy governance tone
  • Approve policies and key decisions
  • Oversee risk and resources
EVIDENCE TO SUPPORT
  • Board notes / agenda
  • Policy approvals
  • Risk review records
WHEN TO ESCALATE

Escalate privacy risks, policy exceptions or resource gaps to Board.

HELPS DECIDE / DO
  • Maintain privacy policies
  • Map personal data and lawful bases
  • Oversee DSAR and notices
EVIDENCE TO SUPPORT
  • Policy documents
  • Data mapping records
  • Notices and responses
WHEN TO ESCALATE

Escalate high-risk processing, third-party concerns or breaches.

HELPS DECIDE / DO
  • Implement security controls
  • Manage access and data protection
  • Support data subject rights
EVIDENCE TO SUPPORT
  • Access logs and reviews
  • Data mapping records
  • Change and release notes
WHEN TO ESCALATE

Escalate incidents, vulnerabilities or control failures.

HELPS DECIDE / DO
  • Collect and use data as per policy
  • Share notices and options
  • Handle customer queries
EVIDENCE TO SUPPORT
  • Consent and notice records
  • Customer communications
  • Query logs
WHEN TO ESCALATE

Escalate unusual requests, complaints or data errors.

HELPS DECIDE / DO
  • Assess and onboard vendors
  • Manage contracts and obligations
  • Monitor performance
EVIDENCE TO SUPPORT
  • Vendor assessments
  • Compliance / DPDP clauses
  • Review and monitoring logs
WHEN TO ESCALATE

Escalate vendor non-compliance or data breach events.

HELPS DECIDE / DO
  • Receive and acknowledge grievances / DSARs
  • Resolve within timelines
  • Communicate outcomes
EVIDENCE TO SUPPORT
  • Grievance register
  • Resolutions records
  • Communications sent
WHEN TO ESCALATE

Escalate unresolved or high-risk grievances within timelines.

The governance loop

Assign. Brief. Act. Review. Evidence. Escalate.

Six stages orbit one idea. Select a stage to see what it means; hover the loop to pause it.

Clear ownership turns policy into practice.

After the last stage the loop returns to the start: escalation feeds back into clearer assignment and briefing.

Keeping governance alive

01

Staff awareness

Conduct regular awareness and training for all relevant staff.

02

Periodic review

Review policies, notices and controls at planned intervals and after changes.

03

Documented decisions

Record key decisions, notices and reasons for accountability and continuity.

04

Escalate routes

Define clear escalation paths within the bank and to the Board as needed.
