Rights of people

The DPDP Act empowers individuals with clear rights over their personal data. Co-operative banks must make these rights easy to exercise, transparent and well-documented.

Rights of peopleKnow your dataConsent & noticesCustomer request workflow
Six rights

What people can ask for.

01

Access information

Ask what personal data is held and how it is used.

02

Correction, completion or update

Ask for inaccurate or incomplete data to be corrected or updated.

03

Erasure under applicable conditions

Ask for data to be erased where the applicable conditions are met.

04

Withdraw consent

Change your mind about a consent previously given.

05

Grievance redressal

Raise a concern and have it addressed through a clear channel.

06

Nominate another person

Name someone to exercise rights on your behalf where applicable.

LEGAL CAVEATRights depend on the applicable legal ground, process and statutory exceptions. Exact requirements should be validated with qualified advisers and current notifications.
Worked example

Phone number correction request

A customer notices that their phone number is wrong. A clear rights workflow helps them submit the request, verifies it, routes it to the right team and records the outcome.

01

Receive

Customer raises a request via branch or email to correct the mobile number.

02

Acknowledge

We acknowledge receipt of the request.

03

Verify identity proportionately

We verify the customer’s identity using proportional measures.

04

Route to the owner

We route the request to the core banking team (owner).

05

Assess the right and exceptions

We confirm the right to rectification and whether any legal exception applies.

06

Respond and record

We update the mobile number, confirm the update to the customer, and record the action taken.
