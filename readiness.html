What a bank needs to get right

Seven things, done well.

The right operating model depends on the bank’s data, systems, legal grounds and applicable sector requirements.
1

Notice

Explain what is collected and why.

2

Consent where required

Capture and respect a valid choice.

3

Purpose

Use data for the stated reason.

4

People’s rights

Make requests understandable and actionable.

5

Security

Protect information against misuse and loss.

6

Vendors

Set expectations and monitor service providers.

7

Retention and response

Delete when no longer needed, handle grievances and respond to incidents.
