DPDP Act in plain language

The DPDP Act and Rules are intended to give people more visibility and control over personal data while setting responsibilities for organisations that decide why and how data is used.

A bank example in three simple roles

Who is who?

Think of it this way

Think of the bank as the owner of the data decision, the processor as a specialist working to instructions, and the person as the individual whose information is involved.
