First 30 days: readiness checklist

A practical 30-day starter roadmap for DPDP readiness. Simple steps your co-operative bank can take now to reduce risk and build a strong foundation.

30-day roadmapProposed readiness support
30-day starter roadmap

Four stages. Thirty days.

Days 1–5

Appoint a sponsor and coordinator

Days 6–12

List systems and data and map vendors

Days 13–20

Review notices and consent, test the rights and grievance route, and check access, logs, backups and patching

Days 21–30

Draft incident contacts and a playbook, prioritize gaps, and agree a roadmap

CAVEATLegal applicability and dates must be checked against current official notifications and qualified legal advice.
Proposed Netravex readiness support

Proposed 10-point readiness scorecard

Our proposed offerings to help your bank get started — subject to confirmation. A proposed assessment framework covering practical areas.

Proposed
  • Data inventory and classification
  • Notices and consent
  • Rights requests (access, correction, erasure)
  • Grievance handling route
  • Data retention and disposal
  • Access and role management
  • Logging and monitoring
  • Backups and recovery
  • Incident response readiness
  • Governance and oversight
PROPOSED — NOT A CERTIFICATIONThis is a proposed assessment framework, not an officially certified scorecard.
